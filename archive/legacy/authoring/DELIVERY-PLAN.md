# Staged material expansion — authoring only

No plugin API, package manifest standard, installer or SolidCraft integration rule is defined here. Those decisions remain with the community. Internal IDs and files are authoring bookkeeping only.

1. **Wood and inventory:** source-backed clear-wood references, grain views and finish variants. First tranche: 16 species, four cuts, six finishes. Additional European/tropical species, engineered panels, knots, veneers and treatment effects require their own evidence. Native/premium acceptance remains open.
2. **Metal alloys:** qualify grade, form, temper/heat treatment, thickness and test conditions from primary producers or standards. Cover carbon/low-alloy/stainless/tool steels, wrought/cast aluminium, titanium, copper/brass/bronze, nickel/cobalt superalloys, magnesium/zinc alloys and solders. Finishes and coatings remain distinct from the substrate. No claim of a complete current market ranking or grade equivalence is made by this plan.
3. **Additive manufacturing:** named polymer/resin grades and fillers, process, nozzle/line width, 0.08/0.12/0.16/0.20/0.28 mm layer variants, top/wall orientation, seams, support contact, ironing/fuzzy skin and postprocessing. Appearance does not establish tensile strength or printed density. Infill alone is not a constitutive model; unsupported combinations are not generated as fictitious products.
4. **Soils, cementitious materials and pastes:** distinguish particle density from dry/wet bulk density, moisture, porosity, compaction, mix, cure and age. Cement powder, paste, mortar and concrete are separate. Visual wetness, cracking and aggregate texture do not silently change mechanical properties.
5. **All 118 elements:** authoritative identity, isotope/allotrope/phase and conditions; distinguish measured, derived, predicted and unknown values. Gases are not solid-body presets. Short-lived superheavy elements must not receive fabricated measured bulk densities or appearances. Commercial-purity engineering grades remain separate.
6. **Legacy reconciliation and quality acceptance:** resolve each old appearance to an explicit substrate/layered construction/blend or an honest unknown. A coating or overlay is not homogeneous bulk. Review finite-scale detail, filtering, flat/curved coupons, movement, CPU/GPU behavior, save/reopen, sources and rights before premium promotion.

An ID list is not delivery. A source citation is not grade identification. A generated image is not a native rendering test. Every stage records these distinctions. The historical v1 release is immutable; later work does not rewrite its evidence.
