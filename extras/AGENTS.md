# Current working rules for extras

1. Work on one `extras/<id>/` folder at a time. Do not rebuild or publish a pack.
2. Do not impose a final category tree, plugin manifest or installer format.
3. Preserve original IDs, scientific qualifiers, licences, units and source pins.
4. Keep physical reference records separate from visual variants; do not auto-assign density.
5. `definition.json` is editable; historical aggregate inputs are not its update authority.
6. `files/` is an independent local working copy; do not hardlink it to another extra or to a backup.
7. Keep `FILES.json` as the original restoration receipt. Never overwrite edited assets during restore.
8. New quality claims require exact-source native evidence, not this migration's checks.
9. Preserve all historical releases and other agents' dirty worktrees.
10. Repository source and discussion are in English. No new ZIP or release without a new owner decision.
